## Team Management in PalcoDesk

PalcoDesk allows you to collaborate with team members across workspaces and projects. Effective team management is essential for successful collaboration.

## User Roles

Workspace members can have different roles that determine their access level:

### Owner
- Full access to all workspace features and settings
- Can manage workspace settings
- Can invite and remove members
- Can delete the workspace

### Member
- Can create and manage projects and tasks
- Can assign tasks to other members
- Cannot modify workspace settings
- Cannot invite or remove members

### Viewer
- Read-only access to workspace content
- Can view projects and tasks
- Cannot create or edit tasks
- Cannot access settings

## Inviting Team Members

To invite someone to your workspace:

<Steps>
<Step>
Navigate to the workspace settings
</Step>
<Step>
Click on the "Members" tab
</Step>
<Step>
Click "Invite Member"
</Step>
<Step>
Enter the email address of the person you want to invite
</Step>
<Step>
Select the appropriate role for the new member
</Step>
<Step>
Click "Send Invitation"
</Step>
</Steps>

The invited person will receive an invitation and can join the workspace after signing up or logging in.

## Managing Team Members

To manage existing team members:

<Steps>
<Step>
Navigate to the workspace settings
</Step>
<Step>
Click on the "Members" tab
</Step>
</Steps>

From the members page, you can:
- View all workspace members and their roles
- Change a member's role
- Remove a member from the workspace
- Reset a member's password (for members without OAuth)

## Managing Invitations

Pending invitations show the status of invited members:
- **Pending**: Invitation sent, user hasn't joined yet
- **Active**: User has joined the workspace

You can:
- Resend invitation emails
- Cancel pending invitations

## Password Reset

Workspace owners can reset passwords for members who signed up with email/password (not OAuth):

<Steps>
<Step>
Navigate to the workspace settings
</Step>
<Step>
Click on the "Members" tab
</Step>
<Step>
Find the member in the list
</Step>
<Step>
Click the options menu and select "Reset Password"
</Step>
</Steps>

The member will receive instructions to set a new password.

## Best Practices

- **Limit owners**: Have 1-2 owners to maintain security
- **Use viewer role**: For stakeholders who need to see progress but not participate
- **Regular audits**: Review member list periodically and remove inactive users
- **Clear roles**: Ensure members understand their access level
