PalcoDesk allows you to customize the appearance to suit your preferences. Currently, the main customization option is the theme (light/dark mode).

## Accessing Appearance Settings

<Steps>
<Step>
Click on your profile picture in the top-right corner
</Step>
<Step>
Select "Settings" from the dropdown
</Step>
<Step>
Click on "Appearance" in the settings sidebar
</Step>
</Steps>

## Theme Options

### Light Mode

The default theme with a white background. Best for well-lit environments and daytime use.

### Dark Mode

A dark theme that's easier on the eyes in low-light conditions. Reduces eye strain during extended use.

## Switching Themes

There are several ways to switch between light and dark mode:

### Method 1: Settings

<Steps>
<Step>
Navigate to Settings > Appearance
</Step>
<Step>
Select your preferred theme
</Step>
</Steps>

### Method 2: Keyboard Shortcut

Press `Ctrl+Shift+D` (or `Cmd+Shift+D` on Mac) to quickly toggle between themes.

### Method 3: System Preference

PalcoDesk can automatically match your operating system preference if available.

## Theme Persistence

Your theme preference is saved and remembered across sessions. When you return to PalcoDesk, it will use your last selected theme.

## Benefits of Dark Mode

- **Reduced Eye Strain**: Less brightness in low-light environments
- **Battery Savings**: On devices with OLED screens, dark pixels use less power
- **Better Night Use**: More comfortable for late-night work sessions
